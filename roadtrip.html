✅ You're Arriving on Wednesday March 18 — Perfect!
The Ark Encounter is open Wednesday–Saturday, 10am–5pm. You'll be overnighting near Williamstown on Monday and Tuesday, then hitting the Ark exactly as it opens Wednesday morning. Afterward, it's a straight 2.5-hour drive south on I-75 to Knoxville. No backtracking!
⏱ Arrival Day Tips
Arrive by 9:45am — the lot fills fast on Wednesdays. Buy tickets online in advance to skip the ticket window. A shuttle tram takes you from the large parking lot to the Ark. Scooters available to rent inside ($43 with attendant). Budget 4–6 hours minimum.
