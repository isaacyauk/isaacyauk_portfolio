✅ New Strategy — Arrive at the Ark on Wednesday March 18, then Continue to Knoxville!
Departing Friday at 1pm gives you 5 full travel days before Wednesday. That's the perfect amount of time to take a leisurely, sightseeing-packed route through some of the most iconic landmarks in the country — Little Bighorn, Devils Tower, Badlands, Mount Rushmore, Wall Drug, Corn Palace — and pull into the Ark Encounter the morning it opens, then drive straight on to Knoxville that same evening. No backtracking. One continuous adventure.

💡 Crazy Horse Memorial — Optional Add-On (Day 2)
If you have extra time between Rushmore and camp, Crazy Horse Memorial is 17 miles southwest of Rushmore. An ongoing mountain carving (larger than Rushmore when complete). $30/vehicle. Blasting happens at 2pm when available. Well worth 1–2 hours.

✅ You're Arriving on Wednesday March 18 — Perfect!
The Ark Encounter is open Wednesday–Saturday, 10am–5pm. You'll be overnighting near Williamstown on Monday and Tuesday, then hitting the Ark exactly as it opens Wednesday morning. Afterward, it's a straight 2.5-hour drive south on I-75 to Knoxville. No backtracking!
⏱ Arrival Day Tips
Arrive by 9:45am — the lot fills fast on Wednesdays. Buy tickets online in advance to skip the ticket window. A shuttle tram takes you from the large parking lot to the Ark. Scooters available to rent inside ($43 with attendant). Budget 4–6 hours minimum.
